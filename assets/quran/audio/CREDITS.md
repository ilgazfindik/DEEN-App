The files are played straight from Wikimedia Commons in the learner's browser; they are not copied into the app, so
they are unchanged and need no adaptation notice. The recordings have no per-ayah timings, so a surah plays as a whole.

The credits are also in `new-surahs-credits.json`, which `scripts/build-surah-pack.mjs` reads to put the reciter and
license under each surah on the path.
